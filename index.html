About Me

I am a marketing student interested in building a career where creativity, communication, and business strategy come together. My previous experience as a project coordinator in the commercial flooring industry helped me develop strong organizational, communication, and problem-solving skills. I am particularly interested in marketing strategy, consumer behavior, and understanding what influences people's decisions. I am also interested in how artificial intelligence can support marketing through content creation, data analysis, customer insights, and more efficient business processes. From a technical perspective, I enjoy learning about data analytics, digital marketing tools, website development, and using technology to solve real-world business problems. I am excited to continue developing my skills and finding ways to combine marketing, technology, and AI in my future career.
